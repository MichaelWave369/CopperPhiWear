# ΦEvidence — V1.0 integration increment
Open the local launcher and choose ΦEvidence. Load a laboratory JSON or a saved laboratory snapshot. Choose two distinct designs, flex or abrasion, exact post-test cycles, exact measurement method, and matching by positive documented conductor mass or length. Declare one candidate and reference specimen ID per line, ideally before measuring outcomes. The tool never auto-pairs or reuses specimens. Plain controls with zero conductor are not eligible for this numeric material-matched contrast; keep them in ΦLaboratory.

Eligibility requires identical material lot and substrate; dimensions within 1 mm; material relative difference <=5% of larger value; complete temperature/humidity at selected readings, within-specimen ranges <=2 °C and <=5 humidity percentage points and between-specimen endpoint medians within the same bounds. These are exploratory choices, not standards. Exact method must identify comparable probe configuration and physical protocol. All selected baseline and endpoint readings must be numeric, with no recorded damage and positive baseline. Medians collapse repeated readings. Contrast is candidate minus reference signed percent change in resistance; smaller signed change is not necessarily better. Excluded pairs, status counts, reasons, unpaired IDs and selected reading IDs remain visible. Other stages/methods are outside this comparison; preserve the full raw register. Instrument uncertainty is not propagated; no inference of significance, independence, construction equivalence or causal effect. Do not choose pairs or tolerances to favor results. Export the descriptive report and keep source JSON separately. Browser and physical validation remain pending; no health or on-body claims.
