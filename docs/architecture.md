# ΦCW-01: PocketMesh Core
Status: concept, not fabricated or validated.

## Construction
Trace the actual hoodie panels onto paper. Do not use a fixed-size template as a cutting pattern. Make an independent removable cotton liner with segmented conductive motifs between two fabric layers. Use garment-rated flexible copper-containing yarn, verified supplier composition and care instructions. Avoid bare wire, foil, solder, tight bands and exposed metal fasteners. Use nonmetallic attachment points. Allow seam, stretch and ventilation space; keep conductors away from seams, zipper, neck and cuffs. Do not join panels electrically. Hood remains nonconductive in V0.1.

| Zone | Motif | Construction |
|---|---|---|
| Back | TriBraid | Open-ended textile motif, not a literal energized bus |
| Torso | PocketMesh | Individual nested-square swatches |
| Sleeves | TriBraid | Short separated motifs, no circumferential loops |
| Shoulders | HexMesh | Optional flexible swatch |
| Hood | None | Defer pending textile review |

## Gates
1. Review material safety and supplier care instructions.
2. Generate paper concepts; check spacing and flexibility with nonconductive thread.
3. Build small off-body swatches. Inspect for abrasion, loose fibers, sharp ends and continuity drift.
4. Build an enclosed, removable liner only after satisfactory swatch inspection. No powered or grounded use; no electrical work, storm exposure, sleeping or PPE use. Do not wear damaged samples.
5. Record results and limitations; do not infer health outcomes from textile data.
