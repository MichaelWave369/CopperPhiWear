# CW-Pilot-001 — detached swatch pilot

Status: PLANNED / NO PHYSICAL DATA. This is an engineering process check, not a medical experiment. Do not modify or wear the hoodie during this pilot.

## 1. Document materials before purchase or construction
Record actual supplier, product, composition, yarn diameter, specified linear resistance, care and handling instructions, substrate lot, garment suitability and available safety information. Do not assume solid wire, bare strands or metal-coated yarn are equivalent. Begin with nonconductive practice thread. Reject loose, sharp, damaged or unsuitable material. Use a suitable low-energy resistance meter and detached test fixture; follow instrument instructions.

## 2. Plan specimens
In ΦWear select pilot, three replicates per design, nominal 100 mm square and seed 369. Enter the actual lots, not placeholders. Export the study manifest, order CSV, seed register and TriBraid reference. Print actual size and verify both 50 mm and 20 mm calibration bars in ΦFabrication. If calibration fails, reprint; do not rescale the recorded design.
The pilot includes 3 plain controls (CW-01-01 through CW-01-03), 3 straight references (CW-02-01 through CW-02-03), and 3 TriBraid specimens (CW-03-01 through CW-03-03). Each must be separately fabricated. These are planned IDs, not evidence. Plain fabric and straight reference are hand-drawn according to docs/wear.md. Straight controls are not automatically mass- or length-matched to braids.

## 3. Prepare and inspect off-body
Use the same substrate and document actual dimensions, conductor mass/length, attachment and terminal arrangement. Use the reference centerlines as a concept, not a weaving or wiring diagram. Explicitly record which paths are connected. Inspect samples for sharp or exposed material, loose strands, damage and unsuitable finishing. Keep samples detached from clothing and people. Stop if equipment or material is unsuitable.

## 4. Measure and preserve observations
Before collecting results, write down instrument and range, fixture/probe placement, lead resistance handling, environmental conditions, a repeatable bend radius, cycle checkpoints and abrasion method. Use a documented method for each specimen and preserve any deviations. Define the comparison and pairing before looking at outcomes where feasible. Record actual readings and instrument uncertainty if documented, using ΦLaboratory. Keep open, failed and missing readings separate; do not substitute zeros. Retain damaged specimens in the record, not in a wearable. No on-body measurements, RF experiments or intentional powering/grounding of textiles.

## 5. Review
Export raw register and descriptive summary. Use ΦEvidence only if positive measured mass/length, environment, method and other criteria match. Nonconductive controls remain in the raw register, not the numeric matched analysis. Inspect all exclusion reasons. This pilot is not statistically powered and cannot establish health effects. If measurement methods or construction are inconsistent, revise the protocol and run a separately identified study rather than silently overwriting the original.

## 6. Archive
Keep the planned manifest, actual construction metadata, raw readings, inspection notes, original reference SVG, calibration records, and report together. Keep local backups. Record date, software commit, instrument and material lot. Do not publish personal or health data. No physical fabrication, wearable suitability or efficacy is claimed until separately documented and reviewed.
