# ΦOptimize V0.7
Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/workbench/optimize.html after starting the existing local server. Choose candidate pattern families, tile size, occupancy grid resolution and optional constraints. The finite grid varies relevant strand counts, turns, depth, ratios and cell counts using V0.2 geometry. The braid families share the same engine; duplicate geometry or equal-objective candidates may occur and are retained. Fractal braid depth is limited to three. No randomness or remote services.

Objectives: minimize centerline length and number of geometric paths; maximize the fraction of grid cells visited by sampled centerlines. Sampling interval is at most a quarter of a grid cell width; approximate occupancy is not exact intersection enumeration or conductor surface area. Resolution affects the metric. Path count is not actual fabrication effort. Compare only at a common size and resolution. Constraints are applied before Pareto selection; a candidate dominates another if no objective is worse and at least one is better. There is no global optimality claim or weighted recommendation. Export full feasible candidates and Pareto front JSON or Pareto CSV. No estimated physical durability, electrical performance, shielding, health or clinical benefit. ΦLaboratory data must be matched by construction, parameters, material, method and test conditions before durability can be included; this milestone intentionally does not automate that inference. Browser UI and physical results not yet tested.
