# Off-body experimental protocol
Compare nine candidate motifs with plain fabric and a straight conductor reference. Keep textile lot, dimensions, substrate, conditioning and measurement technique consistent. At least three independently fabricated swatches per design are suggested for exploratory comparisons; no statistical power is implied. Match total conductor length or mass where possible and report mismatches.

## Record
- ID, pattern version, substrate, material composition, yarn diameter, length, mass, dimensions, temperature and humidity.
- Mark electrical contacts on the construction drawing, including crossing junctions and disconnected segments.
- Resistance (ohms): measure detached samples with a suitable low-energy meter; record probe placement, lead resistance, instrument range and open-circuit results. Never connect meters to worn garments.
- Flex: define repeatable bend radius, cycle count and inspection intervals; document resistance change and damage.
- Abrasion: document method, cycles, exposed fibers and fabric damage.
- Airflow, flexibility and thickness: record equipment and method; do not present subjective impressions as standardized performance.
- RF attenuation: only with a calibrated off-body test fixture and competent operator; record frequency, reference and uncertainty. An open garment is not a shield certification.

## Interpretation
Report individual measurements, median and range. Do not substitute missing readings with zero. Record failed samples and null results. The geometric length metric is not resistance, exposure, biological dose, shielding or clinical efficacy. Health claims require independent evidence; no personal health data should be committed to this public repo.
